Request Account Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your Public Project account and all associated personal data. This page explains the process, what data will be removed, and what may be retained for legal compliance.

How to Request Account Deletion
  1. 1
    Send an email from your registered mobile number's associated account or simply state your registered phone number in the email body.
  2. 2
    Use the subject line: "Delete My Account – Public Project"
  3. 3
    Include your registered phone number and full name as registered on the platform, and your user role (Citizen / Contractor / Investor / Admin).
  4. 4
    We will verify your identity and confirm deletion within 7 business days.
Send your request to: connect@publicproject.co.in
Data Deleted & Retained
Data Type Action Retention Period
Name & Phone Number Deleted Immediately upon confirmation
Submitted Proposals (Citizen) Deleted Within 7 days of request
Progress Photos (Contractor) Deleted Within 7 days of request
Bid Documents (Contractor) Retained 3 years – required for contractual/legal audit trail
Investor Interest Records Retained 3 years – financial compliance
OTP Logs Deleted Within 7 days
Complaint Records (Public) Retained (anonymised) Permanently anonymised – no personal identifier
Important: Once deletion is processed, your account cannot be recovered. Completed public infrastructure projects that you participated in will remain on the platform in anonymised form as part of the civic record. Your personal identity will be fully removed.
